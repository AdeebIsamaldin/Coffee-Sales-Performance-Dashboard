# Coffee-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Designed and developed an interactive dashboard to analyze and visualize the sales performance of a global coffee business. 




Aim ::: To transform raw transactional data into an interactive, visual analytics tool that empowers management to track key performance indicators, identify the top-performing markets like the United States and United Kingdom.

Project Description :

Designed and developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze and visualize the sales performance of a global coffee business. This project involved synthesizing complex sales data into actionable insights, enabling strategic decision-making for sales, marketing, and inventory management.





Key Achievements & Insights:

Quantified Total Sales Performance: Analyzed more 50k Data Sales and the sales pipeline to track revenue streams over time, identifying key monthly trends and seasonal fluctuations to pinpoint peak performance periods.

Identified Top-Performing Markets: Conducted geographical analysis to determine that the United States and United Kingdom were the highest-grossing markets, providing a clear focus for targeted marketing and resource allocation.

Profiled High-Value Customers: Analyzed customer purchase history to identify the top 5 customers by revenue, with the top customer, Alfa Wilmore, generating $371 in total sales. This analysis is crucial for developing VIP programs and retention strategies.

Optimized Product Offerings: Evaluated sales data across four product sizes (0.2 kg, 0.5 kg, 1.0 kg, 2.5 kg) and two roast types (Dark, Medium, Light) to understand product mix performance and inform inventory and production planning.




Technical Skills Demonstrated :

Data Visualization: Created a multi-faceted dashboard (TotalSales, Country_BarChart, Top5Customers) using a variety of charts (bar charts, line graphs, tables).

Data Analysis: Performed sales trend analysis, customer segmentation, and geographical performance analysis.

Dashboard Design: Built an intuitive and user-friendly interface with interactive filters for Order Date and Size, allowing for dynamic data exploration.

Tools: Excel , Data Preparation , Data Visualization​ , Data Cleaning and Preparation , ETL Processes (Extract, Transform, Load)​




Impact :::

This dashboard provides a single source of truth for sales managers, translating raw data into a clear narrative on business performance. It empowers stakeholders to make data-driven decisions to boost revenue, enhance customer loyalty, and streamline product strategy.
